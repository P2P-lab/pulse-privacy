PULSE

Privacy Policy · Effective 19 November 2026

In short: PULSE has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows optional rewarded ads and sells optional cosmetic themes through Google Play. On first launch PULSE asks whether it may use anonymous gameplay statistics and personalised ads. You can change your answer any time in SETTINGS > PRIVACY.

Who we are

PULSE is a mobile game made by P2P (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy or your data, contact us at duetpotatos@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your scores, best streak, settings (sound, music, vibration, theme), unlocked themes, tutorial progress and your privacy answer are stored in a save file on your device. We do not upload it anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Your privacy choice

If you chooseWhat happens
ALLOWAnonymous gameplay statistics are sent to Unity Analytics, and rewarded ads may be personalised.
NO THANKSNo gameplay statistics are sent. Rewarded ads are still available if you choose to watch one, but they are not personalised.

Ads and statistics do not start until you have answered. You can change your answer in SETTINGS > PRIVACY.

Gameplay statistics (Unity Analytics)

Only with ALLOW, PULSE sends events such as: a run started or ended, a circuit completed or failed, the stage reached, score and streak, how many rotations you made, whether a rewarded ad was started or finished, a new best score and a completed purchase (product name only). Unity Analytics also records technical information such as device model, operating system, language, approximate country (derived from the IP address) and a random installation identifier. We use this only to understand how the game is played, balance difficulty and fix problems. We do not use it to identify you and we do not sell it.

Ads (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads)

PULSE has no forced ads. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one (for example to continue a run or get a hint). Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource) and the Unity Ads network. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often you see the same ad, these services process device information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interacted with the ad. Ads are personalised only if you chose ALLOW. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android phones).

Purchases (Google Play)

Themes and Remove Ads are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game receives the product you bought and a purchase token so it can unlock the item and restore it on another device. Unity In-App Purchasing (part of the game) passes this information between the game and Google Play.

Children

PULSE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or privacy laws in US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. You can withdraw consent at any time in SETTINGS > PRIVACY. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days, or contact Unity directly through the policy linked above for data Unity holds. We do not sell your personal information.

Security and retention

Data sent to our service providers is transferred over encrypted connections. Gameplay statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the game and in line with Unity's retention periods.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
