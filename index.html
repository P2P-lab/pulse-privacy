3. Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is primarily stored locally on your device:

  • Game scores, high scores, and best streaks.
  • Gameplay progress and tutorial completion.
  • Unlocked themes and cosmetic items.
  • Sound, music, vibration, and other settings.
  • Privacy preferences and consent choices.

This information is not intentionally uploaded to our own servers. It may be lost if you uninstall the Game, clear its storage, or change devices.

4. Gameplay Analytics

When analytics is enabled and the required legal basis or consent has been obtained, PULSE may send gameplay and diagnostic events to Unity Analytics.

These events may include:

  • Game sessions started and completed.
  • Levels, stages, and challenges reached.
  • Scores, streaks, and gameplay actions.
  • Game failures and completion events.
  • Rewarded advertisements initiated or completed.
  • Purchase events and product identifiers.
  • Technical errors and performance information.

Analytics providers may also process technical information such as installation-specific identifiers, IP addresses, device manufacturer and model, operating system, language, approximate country, app version, and diagnostic information.

We use analytics to understand gameplay, improve difficulty and game balance, diagnose technical problems, and improve the player experience.

5. Advertising

PULSE may offer optional rewarded advertisements through Unity LevelPlay and participating advertising networks, which may include Unity Ads and other mediation partners.

Advertising providers may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • IP addresses and device information.
  • Operating system and app version.
  • Advertising impressions and interactions.
  • Fraud prevention and advertising frequency information.

Where legally required, we request consent before enabling personalized advertising technologies.

If you decline personalized advertising, we will configure advertising requests to use non-personalized or otherwise legally permitted advertising where supported. Non-personalized advertising may still involve technical information necessary to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

You may also be able to reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your device settings.

PULSE may offer optional digital purchases, including cosmetic themes and an option to remove advertisements.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your complete credit card number, banking credentials, or payment card security information.

To validate purchases and unlock purchased content, the Game and its purchase SDK may process:

  • Product identifiers.
  • Purchase status and transaction references.
  • Purchase tokens and verification information.
  • Refund, cancellation, and entitlement status.

We use purchase information to provide purchased content, restore eligible purchases, prevent fraudulent transactions, and provide purchase support.
