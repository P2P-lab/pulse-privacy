PULSE

Privacy Policy · Effective November 20, 2026 · Last Updated November 20, 2026

In short: PULSE does not require a player account. Gameplay progress and settings are primarily stored on your device. Depending on your choices, location, device settings, and the services enabled in the game, Unity Analytics and advertising providers may process gameplay, device, and advertising information. Optional analytics and personalized advertising are separate choices. You can play the core game if you decline those optional activities.

Important distinction: This Privacy Policy explains data practices; it is not a blanket consent to optional processing. Where consent is legally required, PULSE will request it separately through the in-game privacy controls or an applicable consent-management interface.

1. Who We Are

PULSE (the “Game”) is developed and published by Player2Potatos, operating under the developer name P2P (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The legal entity or individual responsible for the Game is the data controller where applicable under privacy law.

For privacy questions and requests, contact duetpotatos@gmail.com.

2. No Player Account

PULSE does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, or precise location to play the core game. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game may store locally:

  • Scores, high scores, streaks, and gameplay progress.
  • Unlocked themes and cosmetic content entitlements cached by the Game.
  • Sound, music, vibration, accessibility, and other settings.
  • Tutorial progress and privacy preferences.

Local save data is generally not uploaded to our own server. It may be lost if you uninstall the Game, clear its app storage, replace your device, or experience device failure. Purchases may be independently verifiable through Google Play, subject to the purchase type and account used.

4. Gameplay Analytics

If you enable gameplay analytics, and the applicable legal basis or consent requirements are satisfied, PULSE may use Unity Analytics to process gameplay and diagnostic events. These may include:

  • Game sessions and session duration.
  • Runs, stages, challenges, completions, and failures.
  • Scores, streaks, and gameplay actions.
  • Technical errors and performance diagnostics.
  • Rewarded-ad events and purchase-related events, if configured in the Game.

Depending on the Unity Analytics version and configuration, Unity or its service providers may also process technical information such as an installation identifier, IP address, device model, operating system, app version, language, and approximate country or region.

We use analytics to understand game performance, improve difficulty and balance, identify errors, and improve the player experience. If you decline optional analytics, the Game is intended to keep analytics collection disabled. Some strictly necessary technical processing may still occur to operate the app or protect it against abuse, where permitted by law.

5. Advertising

PULSE may offer optional rewarded advertisements through Unity LevelPlay. Depending on the mediation configuration, advertisements may be supplied by Unity Ads and other participating advertising networks.

When an ad is requested, displayed, or interacted with, Unity, LevelPlay, and participating networks may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device model, operating system, language, and app version.
  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks, completion events, and frequency information.
  • Fraud-prevention, security, and measurement information.

Personalized advertising is enabled only where the relevant legal requirements, consent choices, and provider settings permit it. If you decline personalized advertising, we will configure ad requests and participating networks to respect that choice. Non-personalized or contextual ads may still involve technical processing needed to deliver an ad, measure it, prevent fraud, or limit repetition. If a compliant non-personalized ad cannot be served, an ad may not be available.

Advertising partners and available ad formats may change as we update our mediation configuration. We will update this Policy when a material change requires additional disclosure.

6. Privacy Choices and Consent

Where offered or legally required, PULSE provides separate choices for:

  • Gameplay analytics: whether optional gameplay and diagnostic events may be collected through Unity Analytics.
  • Personalized advertising: whether participating advertising providers may use permitted information to personalize ads.

You can review or change available choices through SETTINGS > PRIVACY. In regions where a consent-management platform or additional privacy notice is required, the Game may display that interface as well.

Declining optional analytics or personalized advertising does not prevent you from playing the core game. It may reduce or disable certain advertising features. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Some processing may be necessary to provide a feature you request, protect the Game, prevent fraud, comply with law, or maintain app security. We will not describe such processing as optional consent if it relies on a different legal basis.

7. In-App Purchases

PULSE may offer optional digital purchases, such as cosmetic themes or an advertisement-removal purchase. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing, with Unity In-App Purchasing (“Unity IAP”) used as the in-game purchase integration.

Google Play processes payment details. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or banking credentials. The Game and purchase services may process product identifiers, purchase status, transaction references or tokens, validation results, entitlement status, and technical information needed to complete, restore, or support a purchase.

The information processed by Unity IAP can vary by SDK version and configuration. Google Play and Unity may process information under their own privacy notices and terms.
